Linux is a free and open-source operating system kernel created by Linus Torvalds in 1991. It powers millions of devices worldwide, from personal computers and servers to smartphones and embedded systems. Linux is known for its stability, security, and flexibility, making it a preferred choice for developers and enterprises. The Linux community continuously contributes to its development, creating a robust ecosystem of distributions and tools. Its collaborative nature and transparent source code have made it a cornerstone of modern computing.
